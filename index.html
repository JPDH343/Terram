violet says i should start documenting my nightmares,
maybe i'll finally get to the bottom of what they mean.

i don't sleep for weeks on end. i think the last time i slept at all, even just a wink, was 4 or 5 weeks ago. it's frustrating,
i feel like my body is broken. \

in an effort to comfort me about my sleeping insecurities, violet usually tells me the same story or two about mom, how she wouldn't sleep for days at a time- something about her getting older and not needing as much rest. (?) the sentiment is appreciated but the difference between days and weeks and the toll it takes on your body is extreme.  regardless,
i wish i could ask her.

i don't sleep,  because i'm afraid of what happens when i do.
if that makes me a coward, fine. but when i'm asleep, everything is hyperrealistic, and at this point, i'd rather stay awake for weeks rather than continue the recurring dream.

this was what i wrote down the morning after the last time i slept:

04.04.3040 - recent nightmare

i stand up out of bed, confused, staring below at the replica of own limp body laying there, on my back, mouth slightly ajar.

am i an apparition of myself?  a ghost-like image, maybe. but very much alive. and of the corner of my eye, i see him.

the shadow of his large slender body lurking up the stairs to my bedroom doorway. and when he turns the corner into my room, his grinning face, plagued with drool. a dark, sludgy liquid descends from his mouth and sharp teeth.

i try to crawl back into my own body and wake myself up from this  nightmare, but i can't.

his darkness breeds shadows like virus cells multiplying, quickly skittering off of his crooked and bent slouched body. he wears a mask, what looks to me to be made out of a large wolf skull.

the glowing red eyes fixate on my weighted body in the bed. i hear a soft deep moan ripple through the air from his mouth to my ears. he seems, hungry.

i push my back into the dresser beside my bed, gripping the sides with my nails to steady my bones shaking with fear.

his arms jut outwards and he sprints to the body on my bed. he stretches his mouth open, it cricks and creaks like an old door hinge.

i scream, so fucking loud that my throat feels like it's bleeding. but it was silent. i didn't hear it, and it appears he also didn't hear it.

in the same way that i can't hear him very well, i don't think he can really hear me.

it's like the air has become thick, soundproof.

this phenomenon piques my curiosity.

as i stare closely at the air in front of me, looking at the space right above my nose; tiny fractals of light glow like bioluminescent cells in an ocean. millions of them. as i move my hand, the tiny atoms cling to one another and glow. i can draw little figure eights in the air. it's kind of beautiful. these blue, tiny, glowing particles, bringing me a sense of peace and serenity i didn't know i could find.

i look up from my hand, to the stark realization that he has silently gotten much closer to me, and extends his long neck to present his face, 5 inches away from mine.

with his body crouched down to meet my gaze, glowing red eyes burning holes in my hazel oceans, his index finger picks up some of the black sludge from his drooling canines and slams his fist in to the wall beside us. the sludge burps and coagulates in his hand, as if it has a mind of it's own. the bubbling sludge explodes in his hand, his fist becoming the quill of his pen, the drool becoming his ink. painstakingly slowly, he writes
